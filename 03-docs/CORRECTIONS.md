# Corrections to earlier working drafts

These items come from earlier working drafts of this joint project. They are not attributed to any individual. Authoritative fact base: **FACTS.md** (24/24 verified). Proofs: **THEOREMS.md**.

Earlier working-draft statements that the formal check does not support, and the corrected versions:

| # | claim as originally written | status | correct statement |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | "The algebraic closure of the chromatic algebra coincides with S" | **not supported** | the additive closure of $\{1,3,7,9\}$ mod 9 is all of $\mathbb{Z}/9$, reaching $0,3,6\notin S$; because $1\in S$ generates everything |
| R2 | "Complementary cancellation $R+B=0$" | **not supported in $\mathbb{Z}/9$** | $3+7\equiv1$; the correct relation is $v(R)=-v(B)$ in the 2-channel space, where the pairs are $(3,7),(1,9),(6,4),(8,2)$ |
| R3 | "The counterflow balance holds (to 10,000 primes)" | **1.010 %** | $d_k=d_{k+1}$ occurs in 1.010 % of segments and is *suppressed* to 0.64× chance |
| R4 | Conjecture 6: the normalised subscript sequence determines the gaps | **not supported** | 11 898 of 243 008 tuples carry two signatures; the correct law is $g=10\Delta s+\Delta d$ |
| R5 | "Dimension × density is conserved ≈ 0.53" | **not supported** | it equals $1/2-m/x$ identically — a telescoping identity, not a law |
| R6 | "The key decides primality" (unrestricted) | **not supported beyond $p_{k+1}^2$** | the key refutes always and confirms only for $n<p_{k+1}^2$; first failure $7^2=49$ |
| R7 | "The period-4–6 wave" | **artefact** | lag-1 autocorrelation $-0.5047$ from differencing; lags 2–24 inside $\pm0.0032$ |
| R8 | "More dimensions = less compact density of light" | **not supported** | 5D is the commonest rung (30.7 %), 2D the rarest (11.1 %); the order follows the singular series |
